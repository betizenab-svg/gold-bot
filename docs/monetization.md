# Selling the Signals — $0 Budget Playbook

Goal: turn this bot into income without spending anything, as fast as honesty
allows. The product is a **Telegram signal service** — the bot already does
the hard part (signals, entries, targets, live TP/SL updates in threads).

Not financial advice to your subscribers, and never promise profits. Sell
discipline and transparency, not dreams. That is also your only real
marketing advantage against the thousand scam channels.

## The engine of trust: a track record nobody can fake

This setup gives you something almost no signal seller has: **provable
history**. The bot runs on a public GitHub repository; every signal is
committed with a timestamp by GitHub's own servers, and the Telegram thread
shows entry, TP1, TP2, SL, breakeven — wins AND losses, posted by a machine
at the moment they happened, impossible to edit afterwards.

Market that exact fact: "Every signal this bot ever produced is publicly
timestamped. Check the history yourself. We cannot cheat."

## Step-by-step (in order — each step gates the next)

**Milestone 1 — Proof.**
Let the bot run in a free public Telegram channel. Do not sell anything yet.
You need a body of threaded signal history (aim for a few dozen closed
signals) with the risk governor visibly protecting bad days. Pin a message
explaining the R-based system (risk 1-2%, TP1/TP2/breakeven, daily loss
stop). Post a weekly recap: signals, wins, losses, net R. Include the losses
— that is the credibility weapon.

**Milestone 2 — Audience.**
All free channels:
- Short vertical videos (TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels): screen
  recordings of a signal thread going from entry to TP2 — 20 seconds, big
  text, link in bio. Also record the SL days ("our system stopped trading
  after -3R today — this is why members trust it").
- Post the weekly recap in trading groups (Telegram/Facebook/Reddit) where
  self-promotion is allowed. Never spam.
- X/Twitter: post each signal AFTER it closes with the outcome, tagging
  #XAUUSD #gold. Consistency beats virality.

**Milestone 3 — First money (works from Ethiopia, $0 setup).**
Two income lines, both free to start:

1. **VIP channel.** Free channel gets 1-2 signals per week posted late;
   VIP gets everything instantly. Price low at first ($10-15/month
   equivalent) to convert fast, raise later with proof. Collect payment
   manually to start: Telebirr / CBE Birr locally; USDT (TRC-20) or
   Binance Pay internationally. Buyer sends payment screenshot → you send
   the invite link. Manual is fine below ~50 members; automate later with
   a membership bot once revenue exists.
2. **Broker partner link (day one).** Sign up as an introducing
   broker/affiliate with a broker popular in your market (e.g. Exness, XM —
   their programs are free to join). Pin it: "Trade these signals with our
   partner broker." This pays even while all members are free. Disclose it
   openly.

**Milestone 4 — Automation and scale (fund from revenue, still ~$0).**
- Membership bot (InviteMember or similar) to auto-invite/expel VIP members.
- Referral: 3 paying referrals = 1 month free.
- Second product tiers: annual plan, lifetime founder plan (limited seats),
  "copy risk settings" onboarding call.

**Milestone 5 — Sell the machine, not just the signals.**
Once the channel proves demand, the bigger money is the platform itself:
- Setup service: install this bot for other channel owners on their own
  GitHub, their branding ("white label"), one-time fee ($150-500 range).
- Custom builds: other symbols (silver, indices) for a fee.
- Keep your core edge (the tuned parameters) yours.

## Pricing honesty rules (these keep you alive long-term)

- Advertise **R multiples**, not dollars ("+7.2R this month at 1% risk"),
  never "turn $100 into $5000".
- Show the losing weeks unprompted.
- Always: "Signals are for education. Trade at your own risk. Past results
  do not guarantee future results."
- Never handle other people's money. Selling analysis/education keeps you
  out of regulated fund-management territory; managing accounts does not.

## What NOT to spend money on yet

Paid ads, websites, logos, LLCs, verified Myfxbook accounts, TradingView
subscriptions. All of that can come from revenue later. The free channel +
GitHub history + short videos is the whole launch stack.
