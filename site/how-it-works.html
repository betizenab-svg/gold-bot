How it works

Who sends the signals

A program checks gold and forex charts every 5 minutes. When its fixed rules find a setup, it sends a signal to Telegram with an entry price, a stop, two targets and the lot size for your balance. Then it follows the trade and replies in the same thread: entry hit, target 1, stop moved to entry, target 2, or stop hit.

What +R means in money

Every result is counted in R. 1R is the amount you risk on one trade, the money you lose if the stop is hit. If you risk 1% of a $500 account, 1R = $5:

  • +2.25R (both targets hit) = +$11.25
  • -1R (stop hit) = -$5
  • +20R over a month = +$100, or +20% of the account

Results on this site are shown before your broker's costs. The history test below takes spread and slippage off every trade.

How each trade is handled

  • Half the position closes at target 1 and the stop moves to the entry price.
  • Orders that do not open in time are cancelled; trades that go nowhere are closed.
  • New signals pause 30 minutes before big news, and open trades get a warning.
  • Trades are closed before the weekend, when markets can jump at the reopening.
  • Signals stop for the day after a 3R loss, and for the week after a 6R loss.
  • New markets and new methods are first tested privately ("trial") and only appear here once they prove themselves.

Tested on years of history

Strategy and market pairs that lose after costs in this test are switched off automatically.

Risk warning. Trading gold, forex and crypto with leverage can lose you money quickly, including more than you planned if prices jump. Past results, live or tested, do not promise future results. Losing streaks happen even when a method works. Only trade money you can afford to lose, use a demo account first, and never risk more than about 1% per trade.
